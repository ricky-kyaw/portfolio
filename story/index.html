My story

Loikaw

I grew up in Loikaw, a small town in the hills of Myanmar. It is still the most beautiful place I know.

Yangon

I moved to Yangon for school. I was class monitor. My report card never dropped below A*.

COVID sent me home to Loikaw. Then the coup came. My home state was one of the first places the fighting reached.

2020–2022

On paper there is a gap between 2020 and 2022. COVID closed the schools, the coup closed the country, the war closed our door.

I kept a notebook anyway.

When the fighting came closer we were told to leave.

So we left.

2022

I walked back into a classroom after two years without one. I sat my IGCSEs at ALSA International School, a school that no longer exists.

I left with four A*s including Further Pure Maths, top of my school.

Acumen

At Acumen I did a full foundation year in five months while learning to speak English.

I began English in February and finished the year by July, with A*s in Maths and Computer Science.

London 2024

I arrived in London in 2024, speaking English I had owned for less than a year.

Now I lead the AWS Student Builder Group at Kingston, and I compete at ICPC this October.

I am aiming at a master's in Mathematics and Computational Finance at Oxford.

I build things that last — because I know what it feels like when things don't.
