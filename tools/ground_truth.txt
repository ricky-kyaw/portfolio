[story]
Grew up in Loikaw, a small town in the hills of Myanmar; still the most beautiful place I know.
Moved to Yangon for school; class monitor; report card never dropped below A*.
COVID sent me home to Loikaw; then the coup came; my home state was one of the first places the fighting reached.
On paper there is a gap between 2020 and 2022: COVID closed the schools, the coup closed the country, the war closed our door. I kept a notebook anyway.
When the fighting came closer we were told to leave. So we left.
2022: walked back into a classroom after two years without one; sat IGCSEs at ALSA International School, a school that no longer exists; left with four A*s including Further Pure Maths, top of my school.
At Acumen: full foundation year in five months while learning to speak English; began English in February, finished the year by July with A*s in Maths and Computer Science.
Arrived London 2024 speaking English I had owned for less than a year. Now: AWS Student Builder leader, ICPC this October.
Aiming at a master's in Mathematics and Computational Finance at Oxford. I build things that last — because I know what it feels like when things don't.

# Checked first-hand in the repositories on 2026-09-19 and 2026-09-20 (one reader and one sceptic per repository):
Machine learning (machine-learning): two small Python files using numpy, linear_regression.py and LogisticRegression.py, committed on 21 and 22 May 2026 (May 2026); Ricky is the only author. Both learn by gradient descent with one weight and one offset, so each handles a single input number. Linear regression: run on 2026-09-20, given points on the line y = 2x + 1 it prints 2.000000005 and 0.99999998, that is 2.000 and 1.000. Ransom (optiver-chessathon-engine README): Optiver AI Chessathon, September 2026 (Sep 2026). Qualified for the finals ranked 30th (7W 4D 2L: 7 wins, 4 draws, 2 losses), then went out in the last 32. Native binaries (compiled programs) were rejected, so the engine is pure Python with numba. Training data: 106,814,205 positions. The trainer uses PyTorch. README: "built during the competition with Claude Code".
HTTP server (http-server-python): Python standard library only (socket, threading, gzip), no web library. A malformed request gets no reply. CodeCrafters "Build your own HTTP server" challenge. Commits from 30 July to 9 August 2025 (Jul–Aug 2025).

One Project One Month backend (private): Java, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL. Ricky's commits, 2025 to 2026 (2025–2026): developer-profile creation, forgot-password flow, approved project ideas with a reaction-count filter.
Nemo (private, two repositories): a personal money tracker. Nested accounts in different currencies; income, spending and transfers; receipt attachments. The browser never holds the login tokens: a server of the team's own sits between the browser and the API. Three people have written code for it (three committers). Ricky's commits in 2026: first version of accounts, transactions, categories and transfers on the server; the PIN screen, Google sign-in, email verification, lock-out after failed logins and the two-device limit; moved the interface to MudBlazor; extended and repaired the in-between server that teammates started.
